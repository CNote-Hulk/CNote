Marketplace

Posting listings, editing, contacting sellers, favourites, filters, and how transactions work.

  1. 1Go to Community → Marketplace.
  2. 2Click New listing.
  3. 3Fill in the form: title, description, price, condition, category, console type, location (country and city), and optionally a phone number and an OLX link.
  4. 4Add up to 8 photos.
  5. 5Click Post listing.
The listing goes live immediately and is visible to all users.
💡 You must be logged in to post a listing.
The marketplace has four categories:
  1. •Consoles — complete gaming consoles and handheld devices.
  2. •Games — physical game cartridges, discs, and digital codes.
  3. •Accessories — controllers, cables, memory cards, chargers, and other peripherals.
  4. •Parts — individual components intended for repair or modding.
There are five condition levels:
  1. •New — unused, in original packaging.
  2. •Like new — used very little, no visible wear.
  3. •Good — normal signs of use, fully functional.
  4. •Fair — visible wear or minor cosmetic damage, still functional.
  5. •For parts — not fully functional; sold for repair or parts only.
You can add up to 8 photos per listing. Photos are stored as image URLs — you can use links from image hosting services or upload directly if the interface supports it.
💡 More photos significantly increase interest in a listing. Include shots from multiple angles and any visible wear.
  1. 1Open the listing you want to edit (you can find your listings in Home → My Listings or in your public profile).
  2. 2Click the Edit (pencil) button.
  3. 3Update any fields and save.
Only the owner of a listing can edit it.
  1. 1Find the listing in Home → My Listings.
  2. 2Click the Delete (trash) button on the listing card.
  3. 3Confirm the deletion.
Deletion is permanent — the listing cannot be recovered.
In Home → My Listings, click the ✅ Mark as sold button on the listing card. The listing will display a SOLD badge and be excluded from active search results. You can reverse this by clicking the same button again (it becomes ↩️ Mark as unsold).
On a listing's detail page you can contact the seller in three ways:
  1. 1Direct message — send a private message to the seller through the platform's DM system (requires a logged-in account).
  2. 2Phone — if the seller provided a phone number, it is shown on the listing.
  3. 3OLX link — if the seller linked an external OLX listing, you can view it and contact them there.
On any listing, click the heart (♡) icon to add it to your favourites. Click it again to remove it.

Your saved listings are accessible in Home → Favourite Listings.
💡 You must be logged in to save favourites.
In the Marketplace, use the filter panel on the left (or the filter bar on mobile). You can filter by:
  1. •Category (Consoles, Games, Accessories, Parts)
  2. •Condition
  3. •Console type (free text — e.g. "PlayStation 4")
  4. •Country
  5. •City
  6. •Keyword search
All filters are applied server-side — results update when you change the selection.
Use the Sort dropdown in the Marketplace. Available options:
  1. •Newest first — most recently posted listings appear at the top (default).
  2. •Oldest first — oldest listings appear at the top.
  3. •Price: low to high
  4. •Price: high to low
Console Notebook is a classifieds platform — it connects buyers and sellers but does not manage or guarantee any transaction. If you believe you have been scammed:
  1. 1Report the listing using the Report option on the listing page.
  2. 2Contact the Console Notebook team via the contact form with details of the incident.
  3. 3For financial disputes, contact your local consumer protection authority or law enforcement — the platform cannot mediate or reverse transactions.
⚠️ Always meet in a public place for in-person transactions. Use secure payment methods with buyer protection (e.g. PayPal Goods & Services) for remote purchases.
No. Console Notebook does not handle, process, or hold any money. The platform is purely a classifieds board — it connects buyers and sellers who then arrange payment and delivery independently. No transaction fees are charged.
